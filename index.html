Bharat Uday Finserve (BUFL) is an EduFinance organization built around a single conviction — India's rise depends on the quality and reach of its education. We are not a lender alone; we are a full-spectrum partner across the country's learning ecosystem.

Our model brings together funding and value enhancement across five interconnected pillars: school improvement capital, fee financing for parents, domestic student loans, overseas education loans, and vocational and skills loans. Capital alone is not enough — we pair it with school growth programs, teacher and leader development, peer networks, and data-driven monitoring so every rupee deployed multiplies in real educational outcomes.

From metros to Tier-3 towns, we work alongside students, parents, school proprietors, training partners, and impact investors to widen access to quality education — and to make student outcomes, not just disbursals, the metric we are measured by.

WHO CAN APPLY

Requirements

🎓

Students

  • Indian citizen aged 18–35
  • Admission confirmed at a recognized institution
  • Co-applicant (parent or guardian) required above ₹7.5L
  • No minimum income requirement for student
🏫

School Proprietors

  • Privately operated school
  • Minimum 3 years in operation
  • Fee collection proof available
  • No existing loan default history
👨‍👩‍👧

Parents

  • Monthly income above ₹15,000
  • Child enrolled in a recognized institution
  • Valid Aadhaar, PAN, and address proof
  • Age between 25–60 years
