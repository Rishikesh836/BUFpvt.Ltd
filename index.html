WHO WE ARE

Bharat Uday Finserve — Built for Bharat's Learners

Bharat Uday Finserve is a dedicated EduFinance company built around a single conviction — India's rise depends on the quality and reach of its education. We don't view ourselves as a lender alone; we are a full-spectrum partner across the country's learning ecosystem.

Our model brings together funding and value enhancement across five interconnected pillars: domestic higher-education loans, overseas education financing, school improvement capital for affordable private schools, fee financing for parents, and skilling pathways for emerging-economy careers. Capital is one piece — we pair it with school capacity building, teacher development support, and student mentorship so every rupee deployed multiplies in impact.

From metros to Tier-3 towns, we work alongside students, parents, school proprietors, training partners, and impact investors to widen access to quality education — and to make educational outcomes, not just disbursals, the metric we are measured by.

Check Your Eligibility in 60 Seconds

🎓

Students

  • Indian citizen aged 18–35
  • Admission confirmed at a recognized institution
  • Co-applicant (parent or guardian) required above ₹7.5L
  • No minimum income requirement for student
🏫

School Proprietors

  • Privately operated school
  • Minimum 3 years in operation
  • Fee collection proof available
  • No existing loan default history
👨‍👩‍👧

Parents

  • Monthly income above ₹15,000
  • Child enrolled in a recognized institution
  • Valid Aadhaar, PAN, and address proof
  • Age between 25–60 years
