Who We Are

Bharat Uday Finserv (BUFL) is an EduFinance institution built on the principle that India's long-term growth is inseparable from the quality and accessibility of education. We operate not merely as lenders, but as comprehensive partners, delivering integrated financial solutions that strengthen and sustain the nation's educational ecosystem.

Our Philosophy

We operate on a simple yet powerful conviction — education is the foundation of India's growth. BUFL goes beyond traditional lending; we serve as strategic partners to schools, parents, and students, offering comprehensive financial solutions that strengthen the entire learning ecosystem.

Our Approach

Our model integrates funding and value enhancement through five interconnected pillars that strengthen every link in the education chain.

Phystal is BUFL's central philosophy: the integration of physical reach and digital efficiency to serve education finance across Bharat.

Our local presence helps us understand schools, parents, students, and communities on the ground. Our digital workflows make application, underwriting, monitoring, and service faster, clearer, and easier to scale.

Who Can Apply

🎓

Students

  • Indian citizen aged 18-35
  • Admission confirmed at a recognized institution
  • Co-applicant (parent or guardian) required above ₹7.5L
  • No minimum income requirement for student
🏫

School Proprietors

  • Privately operated school
  • Minimum 3 years in operation
  • Fee collection proof available
  • No existing loan default history
👪

Parents

  • Monthly income above ₹15,000
  • Child enrolled in a recognized institution
  • Valid Aadhaar, PAN, and address proof
  • Age between 25-60 years

Not sure if you qualify? Call our loan advisors at 1800-200-UDAY (8329) — Mon to Sat, 9 AM to 6 PM. Free consultation, no obligation.
