Who We Are

Bharat Uday Finserve (BUFL) is a connected financial ecosystem built for Bharat. We bring together digital capability, partner networks, and local presence to make access simpler, faster, and more meaningful for communities and institutions.

Our Platform Approach

We work as an enabler across product verticals, aligning capital pathways, operational support, and on-ground relationships so people and enterprises can move from need to opportunity with clarity.

How We Build

We combine structured products, partner-led distribution, responsible digital workflows, and relationship-led service. The result is an ecosystem that can scale while staying close to the realities of Bharat.

Who Can Apply

🎓

Students

  • Indian citizen aged 18-35
  • Admission confirmed at a recognized institution
  • Co-applicant (parent or guardian) required above ₹7.5L
  • No minimum income requirement for student
🏫

School Proprietors

  • Privately operated school
  • Minimum 3 years in operation
  • Fee collection proof available
  • No existing loan default history
👪

Parents

  • Monthly income above ₹15,000
  • Child enrolled in a recognized institution
  • Valid Aadhaar, PAN, and address proof
  • Age between 25-60 years

Not sure if you qualify? Call our loan advisors at 1800-200-UDAY (8329) — Mon to Sat, 9 AM to 6 PM. Free consultation, no obligation.
