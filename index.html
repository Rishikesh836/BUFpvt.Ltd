WHO WE ARE

Bharat Uday Finserve — Built for Bharat's Learners

Bharat Uday Finserve is an RBI-registered Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) established in 2018, dedicated exclusively to financing education across India. We bridge the gap between aspiration and access, enabling students from Tier-1 to Tier-3 cities to pursue their dreams without financial barriers.

Our commitment is simple: collateral-free, digital-first lending for domestic higher education, overseas studies, school infrastructure development, and skill-based vocational training. We believe quality education should never be hindered by financial constraints.

With a pan-India presence across 15+ states and 200+ cities, we serve thousands of students, parents, and educational institutions. Our transparent, paperless process and dedicated relationship managers ensure every borrower gets personalized support from application to disbursement.
