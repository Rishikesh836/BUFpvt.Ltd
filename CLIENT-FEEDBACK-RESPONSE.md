> **Client feedback:** *The deck/site should not read as "just collateral-free education loans." Position us as a full-spectrum EduFinance partner across student loans, overseas education, school improvement, fee loans, and vocational training — with value enhancement, not just lending.*

- **New headline:** *"Full-Spectrum Education Finance for Bharat"*
- **New mission statement:** *"We partner with schools, parents, and financial institutions to provide full-spectrum education financing — making quality education accessible for every child."*

#### About / Mission section
- Added a formal mission blockquote at the top
- Rewrote the body copy to position BUF as a *"full-spectrum partner across the country's learning ecosystem"*
- Explicit references to capital + capacity building, teacher development, peer networks, and outcomes-based measurement

#### Five-pillar loan product line-up (re-ordered school-first)

| # | Product | Amount | Audience |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **School Improvement Loan** | Up to ₹25 L | School owners — classrooms, water, bathrooms, desks, equipment |
| 2 | **School Fee Loan** | ₹5K – ₹20K | Parents — prevents fee-driven dropouts |
| 3 | **Student Loan** | Up to ₹40 L | Domestic higher education |

| 5 | **Vocational & Skills Loan** | ₹12K – ₹40K | Short-duration skill & certification courses |

#### Three new sections added to make the "value enhancement" theme concrete

| New section | What it covers |

| **School Network & Collaboration** | Local peer clusters (8–12 schools), best-practice exchange, on-the-ground Education Specialists |

#### Footer & marquee

- Footer description rewritten to reflect the full-spectrum + value-enhancement positioning

## 4. NBFC References & Alignment with Broader EduFinance Model

> **Client feedback:** *Remove all NBFC references — the company is not yet registered as an NBFC. Align positioning with the broader education-finance model (reference link shared by Vikas).*

### Action taken — NBFC removal

A site-wide sweep was completed. Every NBFC / RBI / CIN claim was removed. A verification grep across the codebase confirms **zero remaining matches** for any of: *NBFC*, *Non-Banking Financial Company*, *Reserve Bank*, *RBI Registered*, *RBI Regulated*, *CIN U65910MH2018PTC312456*.

| Location | Removed text | Replaced with |
|---|---|---|
| Trust marquee | "RBI Registered NBFC" | "Full-Spectrum EduFinance" pillars |
| About section copy | "RBI-registered Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) established in 2018" | "Dedicated EduFinance organization built around education-led national rise" |
| Info card | "RBI Registered NBFC / CIN: U65910MH2018PTC312456" | "Full-Spectrum EduFinance — Funding + value enhancement, end-to-end" |
| Why-Us feature | "RBI Regulated — Fully compliant, transparent, and trusted since 2018" | "Value Enhancement — Beyond loans, capacity building, mentorship, and outcome support" |
| Director bio (Arjun Singhania) | "Former CTO of a top-10 Indian NBFC" | "Former CTO of a leading Indian financial-services platform" |
| Footer disclaimer | NBFC + RBI + CIN compliance language | "Private limited education-finance company operating across India" |

### Action taken — Alignment with reference EduFinance model

- Adopted the Opportunity-International-style framing of *partners* (schools, parents, FIs, investors) rather than *customers*
- Introduced *Education Specialists*, *peer clusters*, and *3-year school growth journeys* as concrete value-enhancement constructs
- Added a dedicated **Data-Driven Impact** section emphasising accountability, third-party evaluation, and continuous improvement — directly mirroring the reference model's accountability stance
- Introduced four CTAs designed for the model's stakeholder types (currently visible: Financial Institutions and Investors & ESG Capital)

### Section title softened — "Requirements" instead of "Eligibility in 60 Seconds"

Removed an unsubstantiated claim about a "60-second eligibility check" and renamed the section simply to **Requirements**, in line with the no-overclaim direction.

### Sections removed for the same reason (cannot be substantiated yet)

- **Impact in Numbers** section with stat counters (850+ schools, 75,000+ students, ₹650 Cr disbursed, 200+ districts) — removed pending real data
- **Students & Parents CTA card** and **School Owners CTA card** — removed pending product launch readiness

## Responsive & Header Polish (additional work since the brief)

The website is now fully responsive across three tiers:

| Tier | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **Mobile (≤ 480px)** | Single-column layout, full-width buttons, slide-in drawer menu, fluid typography |
| **Tablet (481 – 1024px)** | 2-column grids, slide-in drawer menu, scaled typography |
| **Desktop (≥ 1025px)** | Full grid layouts, inline horizontal navigation, prominent "Apply Now" CTA in the header |

**Header features:** sticky on scroll, subtle shadow, active-link pill highlight, **Apply Now** gold CTA button always visible on the right, hamburger drawer with overlay backdrop on mobile/tablet (closes on link click, overlay tap, or Escape key).

**Accessibility & UX:** touch targets ≥ 44 px, ARIA labels on the hamburger toggle, no horizontal scroll on any screen size, body-scroll lock when the drawer is open.
