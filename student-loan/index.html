Eligibility Criteria

Academic Requirements

  • Admission to a recognized higher education institution
  • Course fee, admission letter, and academic records
  • QI enrollment review for PM Vidyalaxmi eligibility

Co-Applicant & Income

  • Parent or guardian co-applicant usually required
  • Income, banking, and credit history of co-applicant assessed
  • Collateral may depend on lender and amount

Documents Needed

  • Aadhaar, PAN, address proof, and admission letter
  • Fee structure and academic mark sheets
  • Co-applicant income proof and bank statements
