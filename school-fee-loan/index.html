Eligibility Criteria

Income Requirements

  • Monthly household income typically ₹15,000–₹40,000
  • Stable employment, business, or informal cashflow proof
  • Repayment capacity based on EMI affordability

Documents Needed

  • Parent Aadhaar, PAN, and address proof
  • School fee demand note or admission confirmation
  • Bank statement or income proof where available

Institution Requirements

  • Child enrolled in a recognized school
  • Partner-school routes available where signed
  • Term, annual, or admission-fee use cases
