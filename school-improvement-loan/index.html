Eligibility Criteria

Our EFRMs help school operators document the strength of the school, not just the strength of traditional collateral.

Cashflow Requirements

  • Stable fee collections and enrollment records
  • School in operation for at least 3 years preferred
  • No unresolved lender default history

Documents Needed

  • School registration and owner KYC
  • Bank statements and fee receipts
  • Enrollment, staff, and fee structure records

School Type

  • Affordable private unaided schools
  • Monthly fees around ₹500–₹3,000
  • Tier-1, Tier-2, and Tier-3 markets
